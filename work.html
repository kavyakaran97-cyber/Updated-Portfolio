Positioning & messagingSLGPrudent AI

How I rebuilt Prudent AI's website positioning — from persona-led messaging to use-case-led narratives.

Full case study

1 of 7
1 / 7 Open as PDF ↓

Context

Prudent AI builds AI-powered mortgage decisioning for QM and Non-QM lenders. Bootstrapped, 120+ employees, 50+ enterprise customers. Competes against Ocrolus, Laminr, and Friday Harbor.

The old homepage had three problems: it targeted a single persona (TPOs) and missed every other buyer segment, had no mention of a USP or differentiator above the fold, and showed product UI without context — no outcome framing, no proof points.

The site technically had everything a B2B homepage is supposed to have. But the people reading it were confused.

The shift

Before · persona-led Empower your TPOs
After · use-case-led Know if your borrower can repay before the file goes downstream

Outcome over role. One line that a lender could read and immediately place themselves inside.

What I did

  1. Diagnosed the gapAudited existing messaging, analysed how Ocrolus, Laminr, and Friday Harbor positioned themselves, and mapped buyer intent signals from real sales conversations.
  2. Rewrote the narrativeShifted from persona-led to use-case-led. Every headline had to survive the question: would a lender recognise their own Tuesday in this sentence?
  3. Rebuilt page architectureRe-architected the information hierarchy. Added social proof at hero level. Shipped use-case landing pages mapped to active sales plays.

Pipeline generationSLGPrudent AI

How I capitalised on Prudent AI's existing brand and built a demand gen engine to expand into new markets.

Context

When I joined Prudent AI, the company had strong brand awareness — 50+ enterprise customers, a solid reputation with Non-QM lenders, and a product people in the space knew about. What it didn't have was a demand gen engine. No LinkedIn ads, no outbound sequences, no landing pages. Pipeline was under $65K a quarter, driven entirely by referrals and founder-led outreach.

The brand was there. The system to turn that brand into pipeline wasn't.

Phase 1 · Demand gen

Capitalised on existing brand awareness. Built target account lists for QM and Non-QM lenders. Wrote LinkedIn ad copy with use-case messaging. Created dedicated landing pages for each segment.

I owned everything up to the sales handoff.

Phase 2 · ABM (in progress)

Layering in account-based marketing. Researching specific target accounts, mapping buyer personas, writing tailored email sequences and building personalised landing pages.

Started with Non-QM lenders — more receptive to personalised outreach.

How the messaging change resonated

The repositioned website and the demand gen motion reinforced each other. Use-case messaging in LinkedIn ads drove the right lenders to landing pages that spoke their language. Non-QM lenders responded fastest — Prudent AI already had authority in that segment, and the new messaging made the value click immediately. QM lenders followed as targeting expanded.
